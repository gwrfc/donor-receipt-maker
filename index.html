1. Paste your donations

Paste columns in this order: Name · Amount · Donation date · Donation type · Email address. The last two columns are optional for downloads. A header row is optional. CSV also works.

Use YYYY-MM-DD to avoid ambiguity. Amounts are in USD. Rows with the same name and email are combined into one receipt. Matching ignores capitalization and extra spaces. Rows without an email stay separate.
